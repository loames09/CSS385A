A summary of inspiration I'm drawing from to build my game.
Games I am drawing inspiration from include Detention by Red Candle Games, Outer Wilds, and Inscryption (visuals and atmosphere) and Star Fox 64, Geometry Dash, Super Monkey Ball, Trials Fusion (Gameplay elements). I would like to create a game with horror, mystery, storytelling and worldbuilding elements that feels really good to play and is accessible for players of any skill level. 
I would like to explore the options for traversing a level in a structured way like games that are on rails, particularly Star Fox 64. This game has a unique way of balancing guidance and exploration that I would like to expand upon, both to make the gameplay more intuitive and to give more freedom to explore to the player. The levels will feature music that is guided by caesura, crossfades and rhythmic/tempo fluctuations that create variations in pacing. Strong attention would be placed on my part to control these tempos to make their relevance to the game very apparent to the player.
Geometry Dash has a really high skill floor and is pretty punishing (instant failure, twitch gameplay) and I would like my game to differ in this regard. I would also like to explore different genres of music and visuals that are less intense and have a different character. Geometry Dash is constantly fast paced and I feel like the style is somewhat unappealing to some players. The objectives are limited and . I would like to include more input options that that force the player to think creatively and develop familiarity with the levels as they play them more and more.
Artistic Direction - I would like visual elements of the game to be centered around nature, inspired by the natural landscape of Washington in different seasons (in particular, the Cascade Range and the Inland Northwest).
