One paragraph capturing my brainstorm of a game I want to build for this class, including as many details as I can think of.
I want my game to have a strong emphasis on music and sound design. I want to learn how work within either Unity or Godot to
find creative ways to design a game with sound as a central component. 
-   Strong Musical/Sound Design Emphasis.
    - Timing attacks or movements, performing tasks in succession, etc.
    - This should feel good to all players with a comfortable floor
      but with range for more precision
      - The "passing score" is lower, but there is room and incentive
        to improve. Different "tiers" unlock more knowledge of the game's lore.
-   The game will revolve around distinct sequences with musical
    tracks and sound effects. 
    - The objective of the game is to pass all of the sequences 
      to discover the full story.
    - High emphasis on failure and repetition to improve.
    - Easy to restart sequences with little or no backtracking.
    - Seamless integration of level selection "hub" with levels.
    - Make it easy to cancel and move between sequences. Possibility of checkpoints
      or rehearsing smaller chunks of each sequence.
    - Encourage continuous bouts of focus (of increasing duration)
      throughout the game. This is meant to draw from the experience of a performer.
    - Perfection is not attainable, so mistakes are not severely punished. but improvement is encouraged.
-   Musical styles are varied - drawing inspiration from jazz, orchestral and wind band
    instrumentation
-   Unique input utilizing many keyboard keys
    - Not like a typing game, but maybe over the home row.
    - Same keys do different things depending on what actions or
      movements are currently in front of the player in game.
    - More akin to on rails, but with the highest possible level of player control
    - Levels are finite and detailed
    - All inputs should be very comfortable
-   The setting of the game will be a school or some kind of institution. I want to draw from my own life experiences and
    relationships to initialize a simple story.
